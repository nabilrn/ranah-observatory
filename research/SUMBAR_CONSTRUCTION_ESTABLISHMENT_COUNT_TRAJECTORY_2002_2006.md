# Sumatera Barat construction-establishment count trajectory, 2002-2006

## Finding

The official BPS publication `Statistik Tahunan Perusahaan Konstruksi 2002-2006` contains Table 4, `JUMLAH PERUSAHAAN KONSTRUKSI MENURUT PROPINSI / NUMBER OF CONSTRUCTION ESTABLISHMENT BY PROVINCE`.

For Sumatera Barat, the published counts are:

| Year | Establishments |
|---|---:|
| 2002 | 2,779 |
| 2003 | 2,882 |
| 2004 | 2,837 |
| 2005 | 2,435 |
| 2006* | 2,664 |

BPS describes the 2002-2006 series as resulting from Annual Construction Establishment Surveys. The survey coverage includes national construction establishments with construction projects in Indonesia.

## Exact 2003 cross-source binding

The previously recovered `Statistik Konstruksi 2004` qualification table for Sumatera Barat in 2003 reports:

- B: 0
- M1: 16
- M2: 134
- K1: 334
- K2: 1,084
- K3: 1,314

These categories sum to **2,882**, exactly equal to the 2003 Sumatera Barat establishment count in Table 4 of the later annual series.

This exact equality is useful because it establishes that the published 2003 qualification composition and the published annual establishment total reconcile numerically for the same province-year.

It does **not** establish that 2,882 is the exact sampling-frame size used in survey weighting or in the later historical value revision.

## 2005 post-update-period total

The same official annual series reports **2,435** Sumatera Barat construction establishments for 2005.

Relative to 2003:

- count change: **-447**
- percent change: **-15.510062%**

Relative to 2004:

- count change: **-402**
- percent change: **-14.169898%**

This is now a source-native published establishment-count change spanning the period in which separate official BPS evidence documents the end-2005 construction-directory updating.

However, Table 4 labels its measure as the number of construction establishments. It does not label the values as sampling-frame counts, old/new directory totals, respondent counts, expansion-factor denominators, or weights. Therefore the observed decline cannot be silently converted into a frame-change estimate.

## What this checkpoint changes

Confirmed:

- an official published Sumatera Barat construction-establishment trajectory exists for 2002-2006;
- the 2003 total exactly matches the separately recovered qualification-table total;
- a published 2005 Sumatera Barat establishment total is now recovered;
- the published total falls by 447 establishments, or 15.510062%, from 2003 to 2005.

Not confirmed:

- that the published establishment totals equal the survey sampling frame;
- that the 2003-to-2005 decline was caused by the 2005 directory update;
- that the decline produced the historical 2001-2003 construction-value revision;
- the 2005 qualification composition;
- a reproducible old-to-new weighting or expansion bridge.

## Interpretation boundary

Use this series as **published establishment-count evidence**, not as a reconstructed sampling frame.

The mechanism candidate remains plausible but causally unproven. The next high-value evidence is still a period-specific 2005 qualification table or contemporaneous methodology explicitly connecting directory/frame changes to survey weighting or re-estimation.

Preferred target remains `Profil Perusahaan Konstruksi di Luar Pulau Jawa 2005` (`05230.0610`) or another official BPS reproduction of its Sumatera Barat 2005 qualification counts.
