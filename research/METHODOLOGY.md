# Methodology

Ranah Observatory uses staged analysis: source auditing, historical reconstruction, comparison, statistical modeling, and scenario analysis.

Data definitions, geography, units, missingness, and provenance are checked before modeling. Historical values are labeled as observed, derived, reconstructed, estimated, or qualitative evidence. Missing values are not silently filled.

Administrative boundaries are versioned. Historical observations require a source-era geography, an explicit crosswalk, or a separate historical-region identifier.

Model predictions are estimates, not observed facts. Scenario outputs depend on explicit assumptions. Uncertainty and limitations must remain visible.
