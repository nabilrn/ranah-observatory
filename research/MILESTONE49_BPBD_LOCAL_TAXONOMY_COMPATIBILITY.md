# Milestone 49 — BPBD Sumatera Barat local operational taxonomy compatibility

## Question

Can the separately published 2015 Pusdalops PB BPBD Sumatera Barat incident counts be used as an independent same-concept crosscheck for the M42 BNPB/DIBI historical disaster archive?

## Finding

Not yet. The BPBD/Pusdalops publication is useful **independent publication evidence of a local operational incident universe**, but equivalence to the M42 BNPB/DIBI event universe is not established.

The 2015 Pusdalops report records **686 incidents across 21 operational classes**. The four largest classes are:

- Kebakaran: 285
- Longsor: 130
- Angin Kencang: 116
- Banjir: 68

The taxonomy also contains operational/public-safety classes such as `Hanyut/Tenggelam`, `Masuk Sumur`, `Orang Gantung Diri`, `Orang Hilang`, `Orang Tenggelam`, `Penemuan Mayat`, and `Tersambar Petir`.

By contrast, the M42 Sumatera Barat DIBI workbook for 2015 reports **89 `Jumlah Kejadian` observations across 14 explicit district/city rows**. The arithmetic difference is 597, but that number is **not interpretable as missing BNPB events, undercount, error, or release revision**.

## Why the totals cannot be reconciled directly

Historical Perka BNPB 7/2012 describes DIBI Province/Kabupaten/Kota as using the same DIBI application/data basis as DIBI National while permitting local additions, including locally needed data and disaster classifications. That governance model is consistent with a local operational publication having a broader classification universe than the national aggregate.

Therefore:

- a separately published local report is not automatically an independent upstream source from DIBI;
- local operational classes are not automatically one-to-one with M42 disaster-event classes;
- the 686 and 89 totals must remain in separate semantic layers unless a direct taxonomy and lineage crosswalk is evidenced;
- victim/damage values must not be reconciled until their metric definitions, aggregation rules, geography, and source independence are qualified.

## Evidence-access limitation

The official provincial-government PDF was discoverable and its indexed table text was reviewed. Direct PDF retrieval was unavailable during this review session, so M49 freezes a **discovery and compatibility gate**, not a canonical raw-PDF snapshot. No PDF checksum or immutable raw artifact is claimed here.

## Frozen decision

M49 authorizes only the following interpretation:

> Pusdalops BPBD Sumatera Barat 2015 demonstrates a broader local operational incident taxonomy. It does not establish that BNPB/DIBI omitted 597 otherwise-equivalent disaster events.

No BPBD operational incident count is inserted into the canonical BNPB panel, no historical victim/damage metric is promoted, and all M43/M48 fail-closed rules remain intact.

## Next gate

M50 should search BPBD/Pusdalops 2014–2017 materials for explicit victim and physical-impact fields, provenance, and definitions. A future BPBD operational-incident layer should remain separate from the BNPB/DIBI disaster-event layer unless a defensible taxonomy crosswalk and upstream lineage relationship are established.
