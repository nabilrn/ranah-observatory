# Sumbar construction revision period-specific method support

## Why this checkpoint exists

The existing mechanism candidate already established that the end-2005 construction-directory refresh was real, that BPS used directory updating as a survey frame, and that later BPS methodology used company qualification as the basis of expansion factors. The remaining weakness was temporal specificity: the strongest explicit expansion-method wording previously frozen came from later methodology documentation.

This checkpoint tightens that temporal link with a BPS activity report covering 2004-2009. It still does **not** prove that the 2005 directory refresh caused the historical revision of the Sumatera Barat 2001-2003 construction series.

## BPS source

`Kegiatan BPS Tahun 2004-2009 dan Rencana Kegiatan BPS Tahun 2010` / cover `Aktivitas Badan Pusat Statistik 2008` records:

- publication number `02130.0903`;
- catalog number `1202027`;
- ISSN `1907-8862`;
- publisher `Badan Pusat Statistik, Jakarta - Indonesia`;
- preface date `Jakarta, Agustus 2009`.

The accessible artifact is a mirror retaining original `bps.go.id` page markings and embedded BPS publication metadata. Screenshot retrieval was attempted but the remote PDF timed out, so no screenshot-derived claim is used.

## Annual construction-survey method

Section `3.7. Survei Konstruksi Tahunan` documents that the annual construction survey:

- is conducted by sampling;
- uses a combination of **cut-off point** and **stratified PPS sampling**;
- obtains establishment data by interview;
- estimates population characteristics using **company qualification as the basis of the expansion factor**;
- distinguishes qualifications `B`, `M1`, `M2`, `K1`, `K2`, and `K3` by capability to handle construction projects according to contract value.

The same section reports a current sample of 12,199 companies from approximately 80,000 active construction companies. That current sample figure is not retroactively assigned to 2005.

## What becomes stronger

The existing candidate mechanism remains conceptually the same:

`sampling_frame_refresh_plus_qualification_based_expansion_reestimation`

But the evidence strength can now be described more precisely as:

`operationally_plausible_period_method_support_strengthened_causal_revision_link_unproven`

because the report covering the 2004-2009 operational period explicitly documents:

1. annual construction sampling;
2. cut-off point plus stratified PPS selection;
3. qualification-based expansion;
4. 8,168 annual construction respondents in 2005.

The report also uses an approximate active-company universe of 80,000, numerically consistent with the approximate universe recorded in the 2005 directory-updating metadata. That is only a consistency check. It is **not** proof that both statements refer to exactly the same frozen frame or that the refreshed directory was the frame used to recompute 2001-2003.

## What is still missing

A causal revision claim remains blocked because we still do not have:

- a contemporaneous 2005/2006 revision note connecting the revised 2001-2003 values to the directory update;
- proof that the refreshed directory replaced the exact estimation frame used for those historical years;
- old versus new Sumatera Barat qualification-stratum counts;
- old versus new Sumatera Barat weights or expansion factors;
- a reproducible transformation from the earlier values to the revised values.

## Gates

This checkpoint does not authorize silent overwrite, a cross-vintage bridge, a common-ratio backcast, a continuous 1998-2006 trajectory, attribution to the directory update, a causal mechanism claim, or Panel v3 integration of a bridged historical construction trajectory.
