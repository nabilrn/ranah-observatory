# Sumatera Barat construction: SE06 listing boundary in 2006

## Why this matters

The annual construction-series checkpoint recovered a published Sumatera Barat count of **2,664** construction establishments for 2006 (asterisk-marked in the source). That number could not safely be called a sampling-frame size.

A separate official BPS statistical operation now provides an independent same-year benchmark: the **2006 Economic Census (SE06) establishment listing for Sumatera Barat**.

This comparison materially constrains what the annual-series count can mean.

## SE06 construction listing

The publication `Sensus Ekonomi 2006 - Hasil Pendaftaran Perusahaan/Usaha - Sumatera Barat` (publication `05000.0733`, catalogue `9102005.13`) reports establishment listing results from May-June 2006 using KBLI 2005.

Table 12, `Banyaknya Perusahaan/Usaha Menurut Klasifikasi Lapangan Usaha dan Skala Usaha / Number of Establishments by Industrial Classification and Scale of Enterprises`, reports for **Konstruksi / Construction**:

| SE06 scale | Permanent location |
|---|---:|
| Large | 20 |
| Medium | 110 |
| Small | 828 |
| Micro | 3,540 |
| Not classified | 6 |
| **Total** | **4,504** |

The same construction row reports zero non-permanent-location establishments. The five permanent-location components sum exactly to 4,504.

The total is independently reproduced elsewhere in the same BPS-authored artifact:

- Table 14, construction establishments with permanent location by business network: **4,504**;
- Table 1.c, Sumatera Barat construction establishments by regency/municipality and industrial classification: **4,504**.

## Same-year comparison with the annual construction series

`Statistik Tahunan Perusahaan Konstruksi 2002-2006`, Table 4, reports Sumatera Barat 2006 at **2,664***.

Compared with the SE06 listing:

- SE06 listing: **4,504**
- annual-series published count: **2,664***
- difference: **-1,840**
- annual count / SE06 listing: **59.147425%**
- difference as share of SE06 listing: **-40.852575%**

The two figures are therefore not numerically interchangeable.

## What this proves

The same-year comparison establishes that the annual-series `Number of Construction Establishment by Province` value cannot be silently treated as the **full SE06 construction-establishment listing universe** for Sumatera Barat.

This strengthens the earlier decision not to call the annual 2003, 2005, or 2006 establishment totals sampling-frame counts.

## What this does not prove

The difference does **not** by itself establish why the two statistical products differ.

In particular, this checkpoint does not claim that:

- the annual survey excluded all micro establishments;
- the annual survey applied a specific formal/legal-status filter;
- the SE06 listing itself was the exact annual-survey sampling frame;
- 2,664 was the 2006 annual-survey frame size;
- 2,435 was the 2005 annual-survey frame size;
- the difference resulted from the end-2005 construction-directory update;
- the difference explains the historical 2001-2003 construction-value revision.

The operations have different source contexts. SE06 is an economic-census establishment listing. The annual publication describes the Annual Construction Survey as covering national construction establishments with construction projects in Indonesia and publishes survey-derived characteristics. Exact universe alignment, inclusion rules, and frame mapping have not been recovered.

## Consequence for the revision mechanism hypothesis

The finding is useful mainly as a **negative identification constraint**:

> `annual establishment count != full SE06 construction listing universe`

That prevents a tempting but incorrect shortcut in which the annual-series count is relabeled as a census/directories universe or sampling-frame size.

The mechanism candidate remains operationally plausible but causally unproven. The next evidence must identify the actual annual-survey frame semantics, recover Sumatera Barat 2005 qualification composition, or recover comparable old/new frame counts or expansion factors.
