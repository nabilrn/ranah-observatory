# Milestone 4 — Indicator Foundation Completion

Ranah Observatory's initial Research Charter criterion of **40–60 high-value indicators with provenance** is complete when the repository-wide audit reports at least 40 and at most 60 canonical indicators whose observations resolve to provenance records.

The machine-readable completion state is:

`data/manifests/milestone4_indicator_inventory.json`

The permanent regression gate is:

`.github/workflows/milestone4-indicator-audit.yml`

The indicator registry itself is a broader ontology/backlog and may contain more than 60 concepts. Registry size is not used as the milestone-completion count.

The completion batch deliberately preserves source missingness and semantic limits rather than imputing values to reach the target count.
